Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

You vs Everyone is a social comparison app where users answer questions, view aggregate response trends, and unlock optional insights.

Information we collect

  • Account information such as your Google sign-in email, if you sign in.
  • Anonymous guest identifiers if you use guest mode.
  • Your answers to questions.
  • Optional profile details such as age range, gender group, and country.
  • User-submitted questions, if you submit content for review.
  • App activity, diagnostics, analytics events, and crash reports.
  • Advertising-related identifiers if ads are enabled.

How we use information

  • To provide app features such as results, streaks, badges, and insights.
  • To show aggregate community response trends.
  • To review and moderate submitted questions.
  • To improve app quality, reliability, and performance.
  • To prevent abuse, spam, and misuse.
  • To serve or measure ads where applicable.

Aggregate results

Question results are shown as aggregate trends. We do not show your individual answers publicly as personally identifiable records.

Optional demographic insights

Optional demographic details may be used to generate “People Like You” insights. These insights are based on matching response groups and are only shown when enough matching data exists.

Data sharing

We may use service providers such as Firebase, Google Play services, analytics, crash reporting, hosting, and advertising platforms to operate and improve the app.

Account deletion

You can request deletion from inside the app or by visiting: Delete Account.

Contact

For privacy questions, email: youvseveryone.app@gmail.com
